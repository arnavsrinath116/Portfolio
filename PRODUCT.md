# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Code, built together with Claude: Astro + GSAP (ScrollTrigger) + Three.js for the CAD viewer, static hosting. Chosen by the owner on 2026-10-12 over Framer.

## Users

- **Primary:** recruiters, hiring managers and portfolio reviewers for **product design, UX and HMI (human-machine interface)** roles. They skim fast (often under 2 minutes), sometimes from a link in an application, sometimes on a phone. Their job is to decide whether to interview.
- **Secondary:** design peers and mentors at portfolio reviews, who read deeper and judge process and craft.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Arnav Srinath, a designer working across product (physical, with CAD), UX and HMI. It must get a reviewer to the strongest work in seconds and show the whole person behind it. Success means a reviewer understands who Arnav is and what he can do within one screen, reaches a case study within one click, and has an obvious way to make contact.

## Positioning

A designer who works **across the physical and the digital**: Arnav models real objects in CAD and designs the interfaces people use to control them (HMI, UX). Few junior portfolios can show both the object and its interface. The 3D CAD viewer is the proof.

## Operating Context

- Sent as a link with job and internship applications and brought to portfolio reviews.
- Viewed on laptops (most often) and phones.
- CAD models are authored in **Fusion 360** and will need exporting to a web 3D format.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **3 main projects:** a mix of physical and digital work (product, UX, HMI). Names and contents still to be provided.
- **Miscellaneous work:** graphic design, sketches, photography, and other side work.
- Must stay **clean and simple** enough for job review: animation supports the content and never blocks it.
- Undecided: domain name, the specific project titles, whether any work is under NDA.

## Brand Commitments

- Owner-pinned visual direction: **architectural / blueprint line art, dark theme.** Slate-black ground, sand-white lines and text, an orange contrast accent. It is a *design* portfolio with an architectural/blueprint vibe, not an architecture portfolio.
- **Animations are tied to scrolling** (scroll-synced, not time-based autoplay).
- Reference set for the visual standard: see `references/README.md` (Heron AI for colors/layout language, Sahor for flow, Lusion for motion, Igloo for technical touches, Midu for case-study layout).

## Evidence on Hand

- Fusion 360 CAD models (to be exported).
- 3 main projects + miscellaneous graphic design, sketches and photography (files not yet in the repo).
- Nothing else yet: no testimonials, metrics or press. These must not be invented.

## Product Principles

1. **Work first:** the strongest project is reachable within one scroll or one click from anywhere.
2. **Show the object and the interface:** wherever possible, pair the physical thing (CAD, prototype) with the interaction designed for it.
3. **Motion explains, never decorates:** every scroll animation builds understanding (drawing, assembling, exploding, annotating) and the content stays readable without it.
4. **Skimmable, then deep:** every page works as a 30-second skim and a 5-minute read.
5. **The whole person:** sketches, photos and side work show taste and curiosity, but stay one level below the main projects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast; full keyboard navigation; `prefers-reduced-motion` gives a static, fully readable version; the 3D viewer has a still-image fallback and is never required to understand a project.
